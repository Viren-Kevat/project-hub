About QRForge

A free, beautiful QR code generator by ProjectsHub

What is QRForge?

QRForge is a free, instant QR code generator that runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, no tracking, no backend. Generate QR codes for URLs, contacts, emails, and more in seconds.

Why we built it

Most QR generators are slow, ad-heavy, or require an account. We built QRForge to be fast, beautiful, and free — forever. No paywalls, no limits.

9 QR types

URL, PDF, Multi-URL, Contact (vCard), Plain Text, App Download, SMS, Email, and Phone. Each type is thoughtfully designed with relevant defaults and smart icons in the QR center.

Privacy first

All QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — no servers involved in QR creation.
